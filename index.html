FAQ

Questions from the surface

What exactly does Kraken distribute?

Kraken manages tokenized index baskets ("on-chain ETFs") and distributes the exposure and returns of those baskets pro-rata to $KRKN holders, entirely through on-chain contracts.

Do I need to stake or lock my tokens?

No. Distribution is based on your wallet balance. There is no staking contract, lockup, or claim window required to be eligible.

Where are the assets held?

Baskets are composed of on-chain assets held by transparent protocol contracts. Kraken is non-custodial by design — you always retain control of your own $KRKN.

How often are baskets rebalanced?

Each product publishes its own cadence, ranging from continuous to quarterly. Every rebalance is executed and recorded on-chain.

Is this financial advice?

No. Kraken is experimental protocol software. Nothing here is investment advice, and tokenized index products carry risk. Always do your own research.
